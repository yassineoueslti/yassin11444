Privacy Policy

Channel Upload Helper · last updated November 21, 2026

Who uses this app

Channel Upload Helper is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it is connected to. It is not offered to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, in order to:

  • upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, captions and publishing schedule;
  • read the channels' own video statistics and audience retention reports.

It does not access any other person's account or data.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh tokens are kept as private secrets in the owner's own private workspace and are never published. Analytics numbers are kept only in the owner's private notes, to plan future videos.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access stops the app immediately; no further data is read.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the connected YouTube channels' About pages.
